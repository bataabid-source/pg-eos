## Objective
Give the client's inbound request a third answer beside approve/reject: **a date and time**. `ScheduleInbound` (WH_MGR/WH_SUP) sets `expected_at` (+ optional dock, note) on a draft or approved order, emits `wms.inbound.scheduled`; `ApproveInbound` may carry the slot; `CancelInbound` requires a reason that reaches the client. Replicated from the golden slice with `scripts/new-slice.sh wms schedule-inbound`.

## Logistics terms (D-169, §8)
`handover_point` (premium_warehouse · client_site) · `transport_by` (client · premium) · `vehicle_type` (closed list; drives HD-01/02/03) · `labour_by` (client · premium · shared) + `labour_count`. Defaults from the contract, overridable at approve/schedule, proposed by the client on the portal. `transport_by = premium` + `handover_point = client_site` creates a `pickup_delivery` task (SCR-TMS-PICKUP-01) linked by `delivery_task_id`. The `wms.inbound.received` payload carries the four terms.

## Acceptance
A draft ASN scheduled to a future timestamp shows `expected_at`, `scheduled_by`, `scheduled_at`, one `wms.inbound.scheduled` outbox row and one audit row in the same transaction; a past timestamp is rejected (422); rescheduling bumps `version` and writes a second event; approve-with-slot emits both events; cancel without `cancelReason` → 422; the WH_SUP board lists today's appointments by time with vehicle type and labour count; an order approved with `transport_by = premium` from a client site has exactly one linked `pickup_delivery` task; the `received` event payload carries the four terms; a `vehicle_type` outside the closed list → 422; G1, G9, G11, G14 green; the contract (Zod) covers the three commands; Idempotency-Key required on all three.

## Schema (G-01, pre-migration review first)

Event `wms.inbound.scheduled` added to `packages/events/catalog.ts` (Master). Alert rule N-23 seeded (client channels).
