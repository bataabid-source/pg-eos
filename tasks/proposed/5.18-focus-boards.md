## Objective
Give every role one board instead of many screens: a single unified-task model that gathers what a person owes today from all fourteen operation groups, ranks it by due time and severity, and enforces the focus rules (one open item at a time where the rule says so, no board without an empty state, no item without an owner and a due time).
