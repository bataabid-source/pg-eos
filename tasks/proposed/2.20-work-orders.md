## Objective
Make the warehouse work order the assignable, trackable, billable unit for the HD / OF / VA service families: create a work order from a service line, split it into tasks, assign a task to a named worker on a PDA, record start, finish, quantity done and exception reason, roll the done quantity up to the order, and emit the billing event on the **quantity actually done**.

## Source D-blueprint (binding for screens, boards and KPIs)
`docs/package/D-blueprints/12-Warehouse-Work-Orders-VAS.md` — §1 the 39 internal services mapped to 15 task types · §3 the `SCR-WO-01` data model · §4 assignment policies · §5 scenarios · §6 supervisor and manager boards · §7 productivity and quality indicators · §8 screens.

## Schema already present in 13B?
**Yes.** `wms.work_orders` · `wms.work_order_tasks` · `wms.work_order_events` (append-only ledger) · `wms.work_order_task_types` (39 services → 15 task types), the `WO` document series in `platform.counters`, the `wo.sla.*` and `wo.max_active_tasks_per_worker` rows in `platform.thresholds`, and the `wms.wo_rollup_qty_done()` trigger. **No migration is needed for the model itself** — only for anything the slice proves missing, under G-01.

## Acceptance criterion
A work order created from an outbound order line, split into two tasks, assigned to two workers on the PDA and completed with one exception, produces: one `wms.work_orders` row whose `qty_done` equals the sum of its tasks, a complete `wms.work_order_events` timeline for both tasks, one `platform.outbox` event per state change written in the same transaction, and exactly one billing event priced on `qty_done` — with G1, G9, G11 and G18 all green.
